How to play

  • Hold or tap the two yellow pumps, or tap either half of the water, to shoot water from the left and right jets. Keys: A / ← and L / → .
  • A quick tap gives a strong burst. Holding keeps a weak stream going.
  • Float the rings over a peg and let them sink. A ring only goes on when it drops onto the tip from above.
  • Coloured pegs only take rings of their own colour. Keep black rings off the pegs: you can't win while one is on.
  • MEGA (or Space ) throws every loose ring up and empties any peg holding a black ring. It recharges in 8 seconds.
  • Every 5th level is a speed round: land the target number of rings before the clock runs out.
